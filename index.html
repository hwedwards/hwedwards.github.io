03 / A bit about me

Modelling, control and autonomy.

I’m a Robotics Software Engineer at DefendTex, leading flight-dynamics modelling and control-system design.

I develop autonomy software in C++ and ROS 2 on NVIDIA Jetson and i.MX 8 embedded Linux platforms. My work spans Kalman-filter-based state estimation, guidance, SLAM, motion planning and distributed multi-vehicle control.

Previously, I founded SparSafe Helmets, a sports-safety startup focused on reducing brain-injury risk in boxing. Through the Melbourne Accelerator Program, I led stakeholder discovery and iterative product development, using feedback from athletes and coaches to define requirements and validate design decisions.
