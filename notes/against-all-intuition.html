Against all Intuition

An overview of my essay on Henry Ford’s “business as service” philosophy.

A biographical
rabbit hole

A holiday reading journey took me from Benjamin Franklin to John D. Rockefeller and the Wright brothers, then through Sam Walton and more recent business figures.

Henry Ford’s “business as service” philosophy stood out to me. In this essay, I reflect on three counterintuitive principles, and draw comparisons with other entrepreneurs.

Three principles

  1. Price low, then lower.

    Innovate to make it possible.

  2. Pay workers well.

    Tie it to long-term success.

  3. Focus on customers.

    Keep competitors from becoming the centre of attention.

The essay connects these ideas with John D. Rockefeller, Sam Walton, Jeff Bezos and Steve Jobs.
