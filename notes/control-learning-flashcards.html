Keeping control theory close.

A small daily practice for revisiting advanced control and reinforcement learning.

Why I made the cards

After starting in industry, I wanted to keep a few key ideas from university at the forefront of my mind, especially fundamentals from advanced control and reinforcement learning.

I used my old problem sets to make ANKI flashcards, then worked them into the train commute: about ten minutes of practice each morning.

The habit

The aim is simple: keep revisiting the concepts rather than leaving them in old course notes. I shared the flashcard collection in the original post for anyone else who wants to practise.
