Project 04 / Product development & testing

SparSafe Helmets: Product Development & Testing

Building a sports-safety venture through stakeholder discovery, iterative product development and physical testing.

My contribution
Founder · Product & prototype development
Project type
Sports-safety startup
Tools & focus
Stakeholder discovery · Prototyping · Test telemetry

The idea

As an amateur boxer, I wanted to explore ways of addressing head impacts through protective-equipment design.

I founded SparSafe Helmets to explore ways of reducing brain-injury risk in boxing. The earlier prototype, documented as Rocky in my original portfolio, investigated an approach intended to redirect impact energy.

My contribution

Through the Melbourne Accelerator Program, I led stakeholder discovery and iterative product development. Feedback from athletes and coaches helped define requirements and validate design decisions.

I also developed the helmet concept and built a custom test stand with an anatomically representative dummy to investigate different design iterations. The public rocky-telemetry repository contains the C++ firmware for the helmet rig’s embedded data-collection device.

Inside the system

  • Stakeholder discovery. Feedback from athletes and coaches informed requirements and design decisions.
  • Physical prototyping. Successive helmet designs explored redirecting impact energy.
  • Test-stand design. A purpose-built stand and anatomical dummy provided a way to investigate different iterations.
  • Embedded data collection. The rocky-telemetry project supports the data-collection device used on the rig.

Recent development

Heat moulding for fit and foam choice

Inspired by ski-boot liners, I’m exploring heat moulding to make firmer, more energy-absorbent foam compatible with a comfortable fit. Performance on the test rig was still to be evaluated in this update.

Read the heat-moulding update ↗

A more modular test logger

I’m also redesigning the IMU-to-SD sensor system around clearer components: a state machine for device behaviour, reusable buffers and a two-core Pico split between collecting measurements and writing them to storage.

Explore the data-logger design →

Prototype scope

A sports-safety venture connecting stakeholder feedback, iterative physical prototyping and rig-based measurement. Reducing brain-injury risk is the project’s goal, rather than an established clinical result.
